Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 07, 2026

Introduction

LearnKiswahili ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using the LearnKiswahili mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information.

  • No sign-up, login, or account creation is required.
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, age, gender, or profile information.
  • We do not collect precise location data.

The App may automatically collect limited technical information necessary for app functionality and improvement, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • General app usage statistics
  • Crash reports and error diagnostics

2. How We Use Information

Any technical information collected is used only to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Improve the language learning experience
  • Fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The application may display third-party advertisements such as Google AdMob in future versions. Third-party services may process limited technical identifiers according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage

All learning progress is stored locally on the user’s device. We do not store personal learning progress on external servers.

5. In-App Purchases

If premium features or subscriptions are introduced, payments will be processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store payment card or banking information.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for educational purposes and may be used by learners of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, users may stop all app-related data processing simply by uninstalling the application.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
