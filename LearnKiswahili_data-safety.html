Data Safety

LearnKiswahili respects user privacy.

The application does not collect, store, or share personal information. There is no sign-up, login, or account creation required to use the app.

All learning progress and app data are stored locally on the user’s device only. The app does not transmit personal information to external servers or third parties.

Limited technical information such as crash reports or basic device diagnostics may be processed by system services only for improving app stability and performance.

The app is designed to provide a safe, private, and offline-friendly language learning experience.
